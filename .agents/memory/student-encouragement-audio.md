---
name: Student encouragement audio
description: Safety and sourcing rule for Arabic voice encouragement in the student application.
---

Arabic encouragement speech for the child-facing student application must come from licensed recordings or audio explicitly supplied for the project. Do not generate or imitate a child's voice. Non-verbal UI and reward effects may remain locally bundled and optional.

**Why:** The available speech-generation provider does not permit bundled generated audio for end users under 13, and synthetic child-voice sourcing creates consent and licensing risk.

**How to apply:** Keep all sounds behind the persisted mute control. Use the existing non-verbal reward chimes and visual Arabic encouragement until approved voice files are added under the student app's audio assets.
